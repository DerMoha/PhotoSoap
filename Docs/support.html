PhotoSoap

Support

For help with PhotoSoap, email photosoap@brokenmoha.de.

Please include what you were trying to do, what happened instead, your device and operating-system version, the PhotoSoap app version, and whether photo-library access is full, limited, or denied.

PhotoSoap shows only some media

If you chose limited or selected access, the operating system gives PhotoSoap access only to that selection. Use the limited-access banner in PhotoSoap or system Settings to add more media.

A photo was not deleted after swiping left

Swipe left adds the item to your Delete List by default. Open the Delete List and confirm deletion. The operating system may then show a final deletion prompt.

Analytics and privacy

PhotoSoap asks during onboarding whether you want to share community usage totals. You can change this choice in Settings. If analytics are off, PhotoSoap does not collect or upload usage analytics. Read the full Privacy Policy.
