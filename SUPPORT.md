# PhotoSoap Support

For help with PhotoSoap, open a support request at:

https://github.com/DerMoha/PhotoSoap/issues

Please include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- The PhotoSoap app version and build number
- Whether Photos access is Full Access, Selected Photos, or denied

## Common Issues

### PhotoSoap shows only some media

If you chose Selected Photos, iOS gives PhotoSoap access only to that selection. Use the limited-access banner in PhotoSoap or iOS Settings to add more media.

### A photo was not deleted after swiping left

Swipe left adds the item to your Delete List by default. Open the Delete List and confirm deletion. iOS will then show the final system deletion prompt.

### Analytics and privacy

Usage analytics are off by default. You can change this in Settings. If analytics are off, PhotoSoap does not collect or upload usage analytics.
